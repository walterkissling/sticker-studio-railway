# Surface brief: public/index.html

Scope: the whole Sticker Studio app (single page). Mode: Operate. Redesign replacing the print-shop cutting-table world (rejected by Walter 2026-09-15) and the earlier purple/Poppins AI-SaaS world.

Audience: kids (with a parent nearby) in Costa Rica on phones and laptops, English or Spanish. Kids drive describe (typed or dictated), generate, edit, upload, history and the sheet; the parent enters the email and pays ₡5,000 · $10 USD by PayPal. Daily cap: 20 AI designs after an email gate.

Constraints: keep every JS id, data attribute, and function; keep server, payment, and lead logic untouched; single file UI; family-safe copy a kid can read; price and email always visible to the parent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an open illustrated metal lunchbox lying on a gingham tablecloth. The lid and the tray are two hard halves joined by a chrome hinge: the lid is where the sticker sticks, the tray is where the kid builds it, and the latch makes it. Refuses the two-equal-cards SaaS bench, the pastel-blob kids SaaS, and the cream-and-serif kids-book default.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel tin. Ketchup red body with chrome rim and rivets, sky enamel lid interior carrying a printed sunburst, white enamel tray wells recessed with an inner shadow, mustard as the single live/selected accent (active chips, sizes, language, listening mic, in-use thumbs, focus). Chrome hardware: handle, hinge knuckles, latch clasp. Lilita One for the nameplate and compartment titles (tin-print lettering), Baloo 2 for everything a kid reads. Every design is a sticker with a thick white kiss-cut border, slightly tilted, that slaps onto the lid. Rounded-square radii 10 to 22px; nothing pill-shaped except the handle.

STORY: "This is my lunchbox. I say or type my sticker, it sticks on the lid, I put my stickers on a sheet, a grown-up pays ₡5,000."

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chrome handle arc top centre with a riveted mustard nameplate reading Sticker Studio; left of it the one-line promise, right of it the paper price tag (A4 · 8 or 4 stickers · ₡5,000 · $10 USD) and the stickers-left badge. Below, the open box fills the width: tray left (wells for What's your sticker with mic and EN/ES, Style stickers, Size, then the chrome-and-red latch reading Make my sticker, then a small upload strip), a vertical chrome hinge, lid right (sky enamel sunburst with the sticker stuck on it, then New / Edit / Order a sheet and Download). Under 900px the lid sits on top, the hinge runs horizontally, the tray follows.

FORM: Illustrated metal lunchbox / thermos print. Assigned index 6 of the seven grounded candidates (craft table, collector album, locker door, cartoon title card, arcade prize counter, lunchbox, playground mural). Seed key 195852b5. Challengers Metro tiles, cassette J-card, cloud quarry, oscilloscope, cathode gauze all declined. Raises kept: Metro, mustard alone owns every live and selected state; J-card, design and sheet are the lid and tray, hard halves, never two equal cards; Orizuru, the A4 in the sheet builder is one continuous white sheet the kid fills, slots drawn as faint guides on it, not separate cards.

Signature interaction: the sticker slap. A new design lands on the lid at scale 1.2 and -8deg, settles to -3deg with a short overshoot; the same slap places a sticker on the A4 sheet. Latch press drops 2px and darkens the clasp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
